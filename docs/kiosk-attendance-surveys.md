# Kiosk, attendance, session policies, and surveys

The entrance kiosk is an ordinary registered ManagedDevice with `terminal_enabled`, an explicit SessionPolicy, attendance assurance settings, checkout mode, and declared hardware capabilities. The public `/kiosk` route accepts only a valid managed-device credential. It never consumes an existing staff Session, so public requests cannot inherit staff permissions. Staff sign-in creates a Session bound to that device. Server authentication rejects that Session without the same active device identity, and idle or absolute expiry makes it unusable before the browser redirects to the policy destination.

Unregistered browsers use the one default SessionPolicy and are never inserted into `managed_devices`. Sessions snapshot the selected policy ID/version, idle interval, absolute deadline, post-session destination, and optional device ID. User activity extends idle expiry through `POST /auth/activity`, capped by the absolute deadline. Policy or device-policy changes revoke affected active Sessions transactionally.

Attendance is separate from authentication. Password or PIN verification at the kiosk returns a verified Person/Account/assurance result without creating a Session. Check-in locks the enabled Account and Person, prevents two open Visits, and evaluates the person's Lab Rules state. When they must confirm the current version, the check-in without a decision answers 409 `lab_rules_confirmation_required` (role in `blocking` mode) or `lab_rules_confirmation_suggested` (role in `warning` mode) and records nothing. The kiosk then shows the current version (`GET /terminal/lab-rules`, `GET /terminal/lab-rules.pdf`, device-authenticated; only the current effective version is ever served) with a checkbox and repeats the check-in with `confirmLabRulesVersionId`: the confirmation (method `kiosk`) and the admitted Visit commit in one transaction, and an id that is no longer the current version answers 409 `lab_rules_version_outdated`. A `warning` role may choose "Later" (`deferLabRules: true`) and is admitted with admission decision `warning`. A supervised check-in of a `blocking` person without the current confirmation answers 409 `lab_rules_confirmation_required` (they confirm themselves at the kiosk or in the app); `warning` roles are admitted with a warning. Supervised operations use registered attendance permissions; voiding requires `attendance.correct`, an optimistic version, and a reason. Each mutation and its audit event share a transaction.

Completing visitor enrollment on the kiosk counts as arriving, in the enrollment transaction (check-in method `enrollment`, assurance `low`, checked in by the new account, on the enrollment device). When the visitor role asks for the Lab Rules, the visitor must tick "I have read and accept the Lab Rules" (`labRulesAccepted`, 422 otherwise); enrollment records the confirmation (method `enrollment`) of the pinned version and checks them in with admission `admitted`. If another version became current meanwhile, the submission is refused with 409 `lab_rules_changed` and the kiosk starts over. The enrollment result reports `attendance: checked_in`; the visitor does not check in again.

Public presence exposes only first name plus surname initial and Visit ID. The kiosk refetches presence every 10 seconds. Public-tap checkout is available only when the device policy enables it. Detailed completed/voided Visit rows are retained for 90 days by the cleanup command. Before completed Visit detail is deleted, non-identifying daily counts, durations, daily unique counts, and peak occupancy are accumulated in `attendance_daily_statistics`. Exact interactive statistics are limited to a 90-day range.

Surveys (drafts, conditional questions, share links, post-visit invitations, results and CSV export) are documented in [surveys.md](surveys.md). Checkout creates eligible invitations in the Visit transaction.



The web bridge contract is versioned and selected at runtime. Android injects `window.makerspaceDeviceBridgeNative`; the Tauri shell injects a private loopback bridge configuration. Both report capabilities and emit only technical credential events (`nfc`, `qr`, or `barcode`). The web application and Core own resolution and business rules. The current desktop implementation supports PC/SC NFC UID reads; concrete USB drivers and other hardware adapters remain separate deployment work.

## Enrollment photo and on-screen keyboard

The "About you" step of visitor enrollment picks its photo source from the kiosk's effective capabilities (see [Managed devices](managed-devices.md)): `camera` shows "Start camera", `file_upload` shows the upload. After a photo is taken or chosen the visitor positions it under a round mask (drag, arrow keys, zoom slider 1x to 3x) and the visible square is uploaded as a 512x512 JPEG. With neither capability the photo is hidden and not required; the backend enforces this per device.

While the on-screen keyboard is open (the visual viewport is clearly smaller than the layout viewport and a text field has focus) the kiosk scrolls the focused field to the middle of the visible area and sizes its layout to the visible height, so the Next or submit button stays above the keyboard. The "About you" step then uses one column and hides the photo column and the progress header. The Lab Rules PDF modal is sized with `dvh` units to the visible screen; its pages scroll inside the modal while header and close button stay visible.

## Lab Rules in the frontend

- **App.** When the own status (`/auth/me` `laborordnungStatus`) has `actionRequired`, the application shell opens a modal with the current PDF (pdf.js, loaded lazily with the modal), an agreement checkbox and Confirm (`POST /laborordnung/confirmations/me`). In `blocking` mode it cannot be dismissed; in `warning` mode "Later" hides it until the page is loaded again (React state, nothing stored). The dashboard tile and notice link to `/lab-rules/confirm`.
- **`/lab-rules/confirm`.** Standalone page without navigation behind the session (signed-out people go to sign-in and return), the target of the "New Lab Rules" notification and e-mail. It shows the form, or "Nothing to confirm", "Already confirmed" or a no-published-version message, and a thank-you with a link into the app after confirming. A 409 `lab_rules_version_outdated` reloads the status and shows the new revision.
- **Kiosk.** A check-in answered with 409 `lab_rules_confirmation_required` (blocking) or `lab_rules_confirmation_suggested` (warning) opens the rules screen (`/terminal/lab-rules`, `/terminal/lab-rules.pdf`). "Confirm and check in" resubmits the same entries with `confirmLabRulesVersionId`; "Later" (warning only) resubmits with `deferLabRules`. The entries live only in that screen's state and are dropped on Back, after check-in and after 120 s without a touch.
- **Visitor enrollment.** The last step asks for "I have read and accept the Lab Rules" and sends `labRulesAccepted`; 409 `lab_rules_changed` reloads the state and returns to that step with a notice.
- **Staff.** Settings › Lab Rules › Confirmations lists who confirmed which revision and how (`laborordnung.confirmations.read`); the person page shows the latest five. The attendance page no longer has holds or a confirmation queue; assisted check-in of a blocking person who has not confirmed says they must confirm on the tablet or in the app first.
