This page explains how the application is laid out, so you can find your way around quickly.

## The side navigation

The navigation on the left is your map of the application. It is built from your permissions: you only see the groups and pages you are allowed to use, so two people can see different navigation. If you expect a page that is missing, ask someone who manages roles to check your permissions.

- **Dashboard** is your starting page.
- **Workshop**, **Sales & payments**, **Operations**, **Signage** and **Directory** group the pages of one area. Click a group to open or close it; the group of the page you are on opens by itself.
- **Administration** appears when you can see statistics, settings or the audit log.
- **Help**, **About**, the imprint and the privacy policy sit at the bottom.

On a narrow screen, such as a phone, the navigation is hidden. Open it with the menu button in the top left corner; it closes again when you choose a page.

## The header

The dark bar at the top is always available and has these actions on the right:

- **Help** opens the help center with tours and articles.
- **Notifications** shows messages from the team and system reminders. A badge counts the ones you have not read yet.
- **Profile menu** opens a panel with your name and status. From there you can open your profile, switch between the light and the dark theme, and sign out.

Choose **Sign out** when you leave a shared computer. If you signed in at the lab kiosk, the same button returns you to the public kiosk screen.

## The dashboard

The dashboard adapts to what you do in the makerspace. Depending on your role it shows what is coming up for you, open days that need people, tasks that need your attention and a few numbers about recent activity. Everything on it links to the page where you can act.
