Prices are not the same for everyone. They depend on who the customer is, and **Pricing** is where you see and set the rules.

## Pricing groups

A **pricing group** is a set of rates, for example for members or for external customers. A rate says what a machine process costs per hour, or what a material costs per unit. When a job is priced, Core takes the rates of the customer's group.

One group is the **default**. It applies when nothing else decides.

## Who gets which group

- **Role mapping** decides which group each role gets. A person pays the rates of their role.
- An **Organization** can have its own group. It wins over the roles of its members. You change it on the organization's page; the Organizations tab shows the current state.

If a job has no price, the group probably has no rate for that machine process or material. The **Groups & rates** tab shows a red number for gaps like this, so you can fill them in.

You need the pricing permission to see this page and the permission to manage pricing to change rates.

## Price lookup

**Price lookup** answers "what would this cost?". Choose a machine or a material and enter hours or a quantity. Core shows the price in every group side by side. Nothing is saved.

## Where prices show up

A finished job is priced from the customer's group. The price becomes part of an order when you add the job to it. After the order is finalized the price is frozen. Changing a rate later never changes an order that is already final.

Take the _Sales & payments tour_ to see the Pricing page in the app.
