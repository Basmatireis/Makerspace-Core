Core does not issue invoices. Invoices are made by the accounting office through **wiRef**. Core only prepares the request and keeps track of its state.

## When to ask for an invoice

Request an invoice when a customer, usually an organization, should pay by invoice instead of cash or card. The order must be finalized, have a named customer and charge something. You need the permission to manage invoice requests.

You can ask in two places:

- In the payment window, choose **Invoice** instead of a payment method. The order is finalized first and the request opens right after.
- On a finalized order, choose **Request invoice** or **View invoice** if one already exists.

## The steps

1. **Draft.** Fill in the recipient: name, billing address, country, the service period and a description. For an organization you also add a contact person, and a purchase order reference if your lab requires one. A checklist shows what is still missing.
2. **Awaiting approval.** When the checklist is complete, submit the request. An approver reviews it. They can correct details, send it back to you with a comment, or cancel it.
3. **Approved and forwarded to wiRef.** The approver forwards it. Core mails the invoice data to wiRef. The wiRef invoice number can be added now or later.

## Paid by invoice

Once the request is forwarded, the order counts as paid through an "Invoice (wiRef)" payment. You do not record this payment yourself.

From the moment a request is submitted, cash and card payments on that order are blocked, so the invoiced amount cannot change. If the request is returned or cancelled, payments are possible again.

## If something is wrong

A request that was already forwarded can only be undone with evidence from wiRef: ask for the cancellation, then confirm it with the reference of the credit note or correction. That also reverses the invoice payment, so it needs the permission to reverse payments.

Orders that wait for approval or confirmation show up in the **Needs attention** tab of the Orders page.
