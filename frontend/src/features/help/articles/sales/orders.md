Every charge in the makerspace is an **order**. This page explains how an order moves from a finished job to a paid record.

## Where orders come from

There are no counter sales. A sale always belongs to an order, and an order usually starts from a finished job.

- On the **Orders** page, **Review jobs** lists finished jobs that are on no order yet, grouped by customer. Tick the jobs and create the orders; you get one draft per customer.
- **New order** starts an empty draft by hand. Use it for something sold from stock or a custom item.

You need the permission to write orders to create and change them. Review jobs also needs access to jobs.

## The life of an order

1. **Draft.** You can still add, remove and refresh items, and change the customer. The price of a job item comes from the job. If the job changed after you added it, the order shows a warning; use **Refresh prices from the jobs**.
2. **Finalized.** Prices and items are frozen, the jobs are reserved for this order and stock items leave inventory. The full total is now outstanding until it is paid.
3. **Paid.** The payments cover the total. A paid order moves to the Paid tab.

A draft can also be cancelled. You find **Cancel draft** in the menu at the end of the order's row.

## Finalize with or without payment

On a draft the main button is **Finalize and take payment** if you may record payments. It finalizes and records the payment in one step. **Finalize** alone leaves the total open, so the customer can pay later with **Record payment**.

## Changing a finalized order

A finalized order is never edited. If something was wrong, use **Correct order** or **Create replacement draft** in the order's menu on the Orders list. A replacement is a new draft that you change and then commit. At that moment the original is reversed and its payments move over to the replacement.

## The tabs

- **Open** holds drafts and orders that are not paid yet.
- **Needs attention** collects orders that are overdue, changed since billing, waiting for an invoice, partly paid or old drafts.
- **Paid** and **Cancelled** are the finished ones.
