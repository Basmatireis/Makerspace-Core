Every supervisor and trainee has to help staff a minimum number of open days per semester. This article explains how to sign up, how the minimum works and how to leave a day again.

## How many open days?

The minimum depends on your membership. Supervisors and trainees each have their own number in the staffing rules, usually two per semester. If you also have an extra role with a lower minimum, the lowest one applies to you. People who are excused for a semester have no minimum.

The **semester tile** on your dashboard shows your progress and what is still missing. It also tells you the date by which you should be signed up, which is the period's sign-up deadline.

Being signed up is enough to count. You do not have to wait until the day has passed. Depending on the staffing rules, every timeslot you staff counts, or every open day counts once however many timeslots you take.

## Trainees and supervisors

In your first semester you are a **trainee**. You sign up for trainee places, learn how the lab runs and count your open days towards becoming a supervisor. After enough open days as trainee, the lab leadership makes you a supervisor by hand. Nothing happens automatically. Your open days stay counted when you change over.

Supervisors sign up for supervisor places. These matter most: without enough supervisors an open day cannot take place.

Visitors cannot sign up for places.

## Signing up

There are three ways:

1. On the dashboard, the **Next up** tile offers the next free place for you.
2. On the **Open for me** tab of the schedule, every day with a place you may take has a **Join** button.
3. Click a day in the schedule and click **Join** on a free place in the panel.

A day with several timeslots lets you join one timeslot or several. Afterwards your name appears in the place, marked "(you)", and the day is listed under **Mine** and under **Coming up** on your dashboard. Signing up only works for days that are scheduled, in a period that is open for staffing.

## Leaving a day

If you cannot make it, leave early so someone else can take your place. Open the day and click **Leave** next to your name. The place becomes free at once. A message offers **Undo** for a short time if you left by mistake.

Leaving can lower your count below the minimum. The semester tile shows it right away.

## Open days in your calendar

Choose **Calendar subscription** in the profile menu at the top right to create a private link. Add it to your calendar app and your open days appear there and follow every change. Anyone who has the link can see your open days, so keep it to yourself. You can create a new link at any time, which stops the old one, or revoke it.
