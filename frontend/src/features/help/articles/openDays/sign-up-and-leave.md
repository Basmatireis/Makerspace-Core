Every member has to help staff a minimum number of open days per semester. This article explains how to sign up, how the minimum works and how to leave a day again.

## How many open days?

The general minimum is set by the lab, and it is usually two per semester. A role can have its own minimum, for example a lower one. If you hold several roles, the lowest one applies to you. The **semester tile** on your dashboard shows your progress and what is still missing. It also tells you the date by which you should be signed up, which is the period's sign-up deadline.

Being signed up is enough to count. You do not have to wait until the day has passed.

## Trainees and supervisors

In your first semester you are a **trainee**. You sign up for trainee places, learn how the lab runs and count your open days towards becoming a supervisor. After enough open days as trainee, the lab leadership promotes you by hand. Nothing happens automatically. Your open days stay counted when you are promoted.

Supervisors sign up for supervisor places. These matter most: without enough supervisors an open day cannot take place.

## Signing up

There are two ways:

1. On the dashboard, the **Next up** tile offers the next free place for your kind. Click **Sign up as supervisor** or **Sign up as trainee**.
2. Open the day on the **Open Days** page and click **Join** on a free place. You can only join one place per day.

Afterwards your name appears in the place, marked "(you)", and the day is listed under **Coming up** on your dashboard. Signing up only works for days that are scheduled, in a period that is open for staffing.

## Leaving a day

If you cannot make it, leave early so someone else can take your place. Open the day and click **Leave Open Day**, or use the menu of the day in **Coming up** on the dashboard. Confirm the question and your place becomes free again.

Leaving can lower your count below the minimum. The semester tile shows it right away.
