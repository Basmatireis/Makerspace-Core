The calendar subscription puts your open days into the calendar app on your phone or computer. It updates by itself when a day moves, is cancelled or you are assigned or removed.

## Setting it up

1. Open the profile menu at the top right and choose **Calendar subscription**.
2. Click **Create link** and then **Copy**. The link is shown only once.
3. In your calendar app, add a new calendar from a link or URL and paste it.

The calendar lists the open days and shifts you are signed up for, from a month ago into the future. It shows no names of other people.

## Keeping it safe

Anyone who has the link can see your calendar, and a calendar app does not sign in. Do not share the link. If it got out, choose **Create a new link** in the same dialog. The old link stops working at once. **Revoke subscription** switches the subscription off.

Your calendar app decides how often it asks for changes. Some apps wait a few hours.
