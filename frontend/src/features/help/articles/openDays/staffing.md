The **Staffing** page in the People menu shows who staffs open days and who is still missing some. You need the permission to read the supervisor dashboard. Changing things needs more permissions, which this article names where they apply.

## People

The **People** tab lists every supervisor and trainee for one semester, which you choose above the tiles. Each row shows the membership, extra roles, the open days staffed and booked against the minimum, the next open day and the Lab Rules status.

- The **tiles** count people below their minimum, ready for supervisor and so on. Click a tile to filter the table; click it again to clear.
- **Search** finds a person by name. **Filters** narrows the table by status, membership, extra role and Lab Rules; the red number shows how many choices are active.
- Click a column heading to sort. **Export CSV** saves the rows you see.

A person has one of these statuses: _Minimum reached_, _missing_ (with the number of open days still needed), _Ready for supervisor_ (a trainee with enough open days), _Excused_ and _No minimum_.

## The person panel

Click a row to see the person in the panel. It shows the progress, where the minimum comes from (membership, a role exception or an excuse), the next open day, the Lab Rules status and the open days they are signed up for and have staffed. Their own confirmation of the Lab Rules cannot be done here. They confirm in the app or at the tablet.

What you can do depends on your permissions:

- **Assign to an open day** puts the person into a free place they may take (permission to assign people).
- **Make supervisor, trainee or visitor** changes the membership. Every person has exactly one of the three. Making someone a visitor removes their upcoming open days (permission to assign roles).
- **Excuse for this semester** sets the minimum to zero for that semester. You can add a reason, which only people who may see the staffing page can read. **Remove excuse** undoes it (permission to manage open days).
- **Open profile** opens the person's page.

## Rules

The **Rules** tab holds the numbers behind all of this:

- the open days per semester for supervisors and trainees,
- after how many open days as trainee someone is ready for supervisor,
- the warning window: how many days ahead an empty place raises a warning,
- whether every timeslot counts or every open day counts once,
- the default sign-up deadline of a period: its end or two weeks before it, and
- **exceptions** that give people with an extra role a lower minimum. The lowest minimum of a person's roles applies.

Supervisor, Trainee and Visitor are system roles. They cannot be renamed or deleted, but their permissions can be edited under Settings › Roles. Only people who manage open days can change the rules.
