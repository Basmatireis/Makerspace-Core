This article is for people who manage open days. It explains how to plan the days of a period.

## Periods and breaks

On the **Open Days** page, the **Periods** tab lists every period. Select one to see its details, edit it or click **Open schedule**. A new period starts as a **draft**, which only you can see. Move it to **Staffing** when members should sign up, and to **Published** when the dates may appear in the public calendar. Archive a period when it is over. The **Breaks** tab holds the academic breaks. Weekly series skip them.

## Adding open days

On the schedule, click **Add open days**. You can add:

- a **single day** with its date, one or more timeslots, the number of supervisor and trainee places and an optional restriction to extra roles, or
- a **weekly series** that repeats on one weekday between two dates. Holidays and breaks are skipped unless you include a date on purpose.

Open days on the same date must not overlap, and the timeslots of one day must follow each other without overlapping.

## The working copy and the save bar

Everything you change on the schedule stays in a working copy first. A bar at the bottom counts the unsaved changes and offers **Undo last change**, **Discard** and **Save schedule**. Nothing reaches other people until you save, and then all changes are saved together or not at all.

When you save, people whose timeslot changed, who lost their day or whose day was cancelled get a message. In a draft period nobody is told, because nobody has signed up yet.

## Editing days

Click a day and use the pencil in its panel to change the date, the timeslots, the places or the restriction. To change several days at once, click **Select**, tick the days and use **Edit**. The menu of a day also offers **Duplicate to next week** and **Cancel open day**. In a draft period the day is deleted instead. A cancelled day stays visible, but nobody can sign up for it.

## Series

A day that belongs to a series says so in its panel. **Edit series** changes the template for all days from a date you choose. Days in the past and cancelled days stay as they are. If you shorten the series, days without assignments disappear and the others are cancelled. If you edit a single day of a series, it leaves the series. **Follow the series again** takes it back. **Cancel the rest of the series** ends the series before the day you are looking at.

## Keeping an eye on staffing

The **Short-staffed** tab lists the days that still need supervisors. The **Staffing** page shows each person against their minimum and lets you assign people, change their membership or excuse them for a semester.
