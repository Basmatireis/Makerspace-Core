This article is for people who may put others into open day places. The **Assign** button appears only if you have the permission to assign people. If you do not see it, you cannot assign others, but you can still sign yourself up.

## Reading the staffing of a day

Click a day on the **Open Days** schedule. Each timeslot shows its supervisor and trainee places with a counter such as 2 of 3, and below it who is signed up, if you may see names. Without that permission you see only yourself and the number of filled places.

A timeslot is short-staffed when it has too few supervisors. Empty trainee places do not matter for that. The **Short-staffed** tab lists the days that need attention. On the **Staffing** page you see who is still below their minimum.

## Assigning a person

1. In the panel of the day, find a free place and click **Assign**.
2. Search by name if the list is long.
3. Click the name of the person.

The person is assigned at once and the counter goes up. The list only shows people who are eligible for that place and who are not already in that timeslot. People below their minimum come first. If the person is no longer eligible or the place was just filled, the page tells you so and you can pick someone else.

You can also assign from the **Staffing** page: select a person and click **Assign to an open day**.

## Removing someone

Click the small cross next to a name. The place becomes free again. Be careful with people who count on their open days for the semester minimum, and tell them if you remove them. People who are removed by someone else get a message.

## Good to know

- You cannot assign people to cancelled days or in archived periods.
- A person can only hold one place per timeslot.
- Losing a role later does not remove existing assignments.
- Every assignment is recorded in the audit log.
