This article is for people who may put others into open day places. The **Assign person** button appears only if you have the permission to assign people. If you do not see it, you cannot assign others, but you can still sign yourself up.

## Reading the staffing of a day

Open a day from the **Open Days** page. Each kind of place is shown as a card with a counter such as 2/3. Green means all places are taken, red means some are missing. Below the counter you see who is signed up, if you may see names. Without that permission you see only yourself and the number of filled places.

A day is short-staffed when it has too few supervisors. Empty trainee places do not matter for that. The periods table on the **Open Days** page shows how many supervisor places are still open in each period, which is a good way to find days that need attention.

## Assigning a person

1. On the day, find a card with free places and click **Assign person**.
2. Search by name if the list is long.
3. Click the name of the person.

The person is assigned at once and the counter goes up. The list only shows people who are eligible for that kind of place and who are not already on that day. Members without a login can be assigned too. If the person is no longer eligible or the place was just filled, the page tells you so and you can pick someone else.

## Removing someone

Click the small cross next to a name and confirm. The place becomes free again. Be careful with people who count on their open days for the semester minimum, and tell them if you remove them.

## Good to know

- You cannot assign people to cancelled days or in archived periods.
- A person can only hold one place per day.
- Losing a role later does not remove existing assignments.
- Every assignment is recorded in the audit log.
