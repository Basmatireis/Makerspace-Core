Open days are the days when the makerspace opens its doors to guests. Guests are students who want to see the lab and try things out, and they come only on open days. The lab has no front desk and no employees, so the supervisors run every open day themselves.

## Periods

Open days are grouped into **periods**, usually one per semester. A period has a name, start and end dates and an optional **sign-up deadline**. The deadline is the date by which every member should have signed up for their minimum number of open days. Without one, the staffing rules decide: the end of the period, or two weeks before it.

A period has a status:

- **Draft** is being prepared. Only people who manage open days see it, and nobody can sign up yet.
- **Staffing** is visible to members, who can sign up for days.
- **Published** works the same for members, and the dates are also shown to guests in the public calendar. The calendar shows only dates and times, never names.
- **Archived** is finished and can no longer be changed.

## Where to find them

People who manage open days land on the **Open Days** page with the list of periods. The **Breaks** tab holds the academic breaks. Select a period and click **Open schedule**.

Everyone else goes straight to the schedule of the current period and can switch to another visible period in the page header.

The schedule has the sections **Upcoming**, **Short-staffed** (for people who manage open days), **Open for me** and **Mine** (for people who sign up) and **Past**. Show the days as a list or as a calendar. Click a day to see its places and who is signed up in the panel beside the list. On a narrow screen the panel opens as a sheet.

The dashboard shows the same information from your point of view: **Next up** tells you about your next open day, **Coming up** lists all days you have signed up for, and the semester tile counts your open days.

## Timeslots and places

An open day can have several **timeslots**, for example a morning and an afternoon shift. Each timeslot has its own places:

- **Supervisor** places are for members who are supervisors.
- **Trainee** places are for members in their first semester. They learn alongside the supervisors.

A place can be limited to people with a certain extra role, for example a training. Only supervisor places decide whether an open day can take place. A timeslot with too few supervisors is marked as short-staffed. Empty trainee places never raise a warning.

If a day is cancelled, it stays visible but nobody can sign up or be assigned any more.
