The help center collects everything that explains the application. Open it with the **Help** button in the header or the **Help** entry in the side navigation.

## Tours and workflow demos

There are two kinds of guides:

- **Tours** point at parts of the real application and explain what they are for. They change nothing. The welcome tour is an example.
- **Workflow demos** walk you through a task from start to finish, for example creating a record. They run in **demo mode**.

Each guide shows how long it takes, and the help center marks the ones you have completed.

## Demo mode

While a workflow demo runs, the application shows sample data in your browser. You can click, type and save as you would for real, but **nothing is saved and nothing reaches the server**. A banner at the top of the page tells you that demo mode is on. When the demo ends, or when you choose **Leave demo** in the banner, the sample data disappears and you are back where you started.

## Using a guide

- **Next** and **Back** move between steps. **Close tour** or the Escape key ends the guide at any time.
- Some steps ask you to click the highlighted element yourself; the guide moves on when you do.
- The highlighted area is dimmed around, but you can still use the page when a step asks you to.
- On a narrow screen, parts of the application that are hidden are described in a centered box instead of being highlighted.

## Starting tours again

A tour you have completed or dismissed does not pop up again, but you can start any guide from the help center whenever you like. To bring back the first-time offers, choose **Show the tours again** at the top of the help center.

## Search

The search field in the help center looks through the titles and summaries of all guides and articles that apply to you. Only content for pages you have access to is listed.
