The **Machines** page lists everything that can do work in the makerspace. Open it under **Workshop > Machines**.

## The machine list

Each row shows one machine:

- **Availability** is worked out from what the machine is doing: available, in use, in maintenance or retired. Retired machines are hidden until you filter for them.
- **Activity** shows the job or run that is running now, or when the machine was last used.
- **Jobs** and **Failure rate** summarize its history.
- **Connector** tells you whether a printer connection is working. It stays empty for machines without one.
- The last column flags machines that need attention, such as an offline connector, a printer error, maintenance or runs that still need a job.

Search by name, process or status. The filter button narrows the list by availability, process, collection and connector state.

## The machine panel

On a wide screen, click a machine to see its panel next to the list. It shows the process, whether runs are collected automatically or entered by hand, the connector state, the latest jobs and, for printers, the live status and the mounted spools. From the panel you can start a **New job** for that machine. On a narrow screen a click opens the machine page instead.

## Processes and printers

The tabs at the top hold more:

- **Processes** are the kinds of work, such as 3D printing or laser cutting. A process decides which materials fit and what a run records.
- **Printers** collects the 3D printer connections and their notifications.

People with the permission to manage machines can also add machines here with **Add machine**. A guided setup walks through the printer connection.

## Automatic and manual collection

A machine with **automatic** collection receives its runs from a connector. They show up in the strip of unassigned runs on the Jobs page until someone claims them. A **manual** machine only has runs that people enter.
