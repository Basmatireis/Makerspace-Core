The inventory knows what material the makerspace has, what it cost and what jobs have used. Open it under **Workshop > Inventory**.

## The tabs

- **Articles** lists everything in stock: filament, consumables such as plywood, and parts. Each row shows the quantity, the unit and the stock state: in stock, low or empty.
- **Spools** lists filament spools one by one.
- **Categories** defines the material types, their variants and which machines use them.
- **Recycle bin** holds retired spools, deleted articles and deleted ledger entries.

On a wide screen, click a row to see its details next to the list. The article page also shows the full ledger, which is the history of every delivery, use and correction.

## Receiving stock

When a delivery arrives, use **Receive stock**. Choose an existing article or create a new one, enter the number of packages, the size of one package and the total price. The price sets what the stock is worth. For filament you can put the delivery in storage or directly onto new spools, one per package.

You need the permission to manage the inventory to receive stock. Without it you can look but not change.

## Filament spools

A **spool** is one unit of filament. It has a label such as S-PLA-BASIC-BLACK-01, a starting weight and the amount left. You can:

- **Register** a spool from loose stock or from a purchase.
- **Weigh** it to correct the amount left.
- **Mount** it on a printer slot. Printers that read tags can also detect it.
- **Retire** it when it is empty or thrown away.

Material that a print uses is booked on the spool that was loaded. If a spool ran out in the middle of a print, the spool shows a warning. Nothing is booked or retired automatically, so weigh or retire it yourself.

## Low stock and removal

An article switches to low stock when it falls below its threshold. Deleting an article or retiring a spool moves it to the recycle bin; **Restore** brings it back. **Delete permanently** is only offered to people who may do it.
