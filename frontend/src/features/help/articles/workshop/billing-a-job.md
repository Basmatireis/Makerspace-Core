Every job has a recipient, and the recipient pays for it. This article shows where to see the price and how a job reaches an order.

## Where the price comes from

The price is worked out when the job is created. It adds the machine time and the material used, at the rates of the recipient's pricing group. The **Price & billing** card on the job page shows the result. If a rate is missing, the price is marked **Incomplete**, and the job is flagged as needing attention.

You can recalculate the price after a correction. People with the right permission can also adjust it by hand.

## Billing status

The **Billing** column of the jobs table says where a job stands:

- **Not billed** means the job has ended but is on no order yet.
- An order number means the job is on that order.
- **Order outdated** means the job changed after it was added to the order.
- **Price incomplete** means a rate is missing.

Running jobs show nothing yet.

## Putting a job on an order

Once a job has finished, **Add to order** on the job page adds it to the recipient's draft order, or starts a new draft. The order opens afterwards. Adding jobs to orders needs permission to write orders.

From there, orders and payments take over. They are described in the Sales & payments help articles.
